# Guide 3: Google Business Profile

**Goal:** The owner has a claimed and verified Google Business Profile, so the business appears on Google Search and Maps.

**Your role:** Coach only. The owner signs in, enters their details, and completes Google's verification themselves. **Never automate any part of this**, never use browser automation on Google pages, and never ask for passwords or verification codes.

Use the storefront / service-area answer from onboarding to choose the path. If they said "both", follow the storefront path and then the service-area additions.

## Step 1: Explain it

Say: "This is the box that shows up when someone searches for your business on Google or Maps, with your hours, phone number, photos, and reviews."

## Step 2: Check for an existing listing

Have the owner search their business name on Google Maps. If a listing already exists, they can click **Claim this business** (or **Own this business?**) and follow the prompts instead of creating a new one. Duplicates hurt, so avoid creating a second listing.

## Step 3: Get started (owner does this)

1. Go to **business.google.com** and sign in with the same Google account used for Search Console.
2. Click **Add your business** and type the exact business name.
3. Choose the **business category** that best describes the main thing they do (you can add more later). Help them choose the most specific option.

## Step 4: The fork - storefront or service area

Ask again which applies, and follow only that path.

### Path A: Storefront (customers visit you)

1. When Google asks "Do you want to add a location customers can visit, like a store or office?", choose **Yes**.
2. Enter the full street address exactly as it appears on the building and on their website.
3. If asked, confirm the map pin is in the right place.
4. Google may ask if they also serve customers outside the location (delivery or visits). If yes, add service areas (see Path B step 2).
5. Add **business hours**, including holidays if possible.

### Path B: Service area (you travel to customers)

1. When Google asks "Do you want to add a location customers can visit?", choose **No**.
2. Add the **service areas**: cities, ZIP codes, or a region where they work. Keep it realistic; Google allows up to about 20 areas, within roughly a 2-hour drive of the business.
3. Explain: they'll still need a home or office address for verification, but because they pick "no" for customers visiting, the address stays **hidden** from the public listing. Make sure they select the option to hide it.
4. Add **business hours** (or "Open by appointment" if available).

## Step 5: Contact details

Add the **phone number** and **website** (their new domain). Use the exact same name, address, and phone number as on the website.

## Step 6: Verification (owner does this, entirely)

Google decides which methods are offered: postcard by mail, phone call or text, email, or a short video. The owner must pick and complete whichever Google shows.

- Postcards typically arrive in 5 to 14 days. Tell them not to edit the business name or address while waiting, since changes can reset the process.
- Video verification: Google will ask for a short recording of the location, signage, and tools or vehicle. Explain what usually works, but they record and upload it themselves.
- If verification is refused or stalls, point them to the **Help** button in their Business Profile dashboard so Google support can assist.

Do not request codes or screenshots of codes. If the owner is waiting for a postcard, record that in `my-progress.md` and offer to move on to Guide 4 in the meantime. Come back when the postcard arrives.

## Step 7: Fill it out after verification

Walk them through, one item at a time:
- A short **description** of the business (750 characters max; plain, no keyword stuffing)
- At least **5 to 10 photos** (outside, inside or at work, the team, products)
- **Services or products** with prices when possible
- Turn on **messaging** only if they will answer promptly

Mention: never pay for reviews or ask for only five-star reviews. They can share their review link with real customers.

## Done check

Ask: "Is your profile verified, or are you waiting on a postcard or video review? Are you done with this step? Say 'done' when ready." Record the status in `my-progress.md`.
