# Guide 1: Website and Domain (Namecheap)

**Goal:** The owner has a domain (their web address, like `yourbusiness.com`) and a live website.

**Your role:** Coach only. The owner creates the Namecheap account, picks the name, and pays. Never ask for passwords or payment details.

Check the Namecheap prices and screens as you go; they change. If what the owner sees doesn't match, ask them to describe it and adapt.

## Step 1: Explain what we're doing

Say: "A *domain* is your web address. A *website* is the page people see when they visit it. We'll buy the address first, then set up the site."

## Step 2: Pick the name

Help them brainstorm. Good domains are short, easy to say aloud, and match the business name. Offer 3 to 5 options based on the business name and location from onboarding (for example `bobsplumbing.com`, `bobsplumbingdenver.com`). Prefer `.com`. Avoid hyphens and numbers.

Ask them to pick their top choice and a backup.

## Step 3: Create a Namecheap account (owner does this)

Tell them:
1. Go to **namecheap.com** in their browser.
2. Click **Sign Up** (top right) and create an account using a business email they check regularly.
3. Use a strong, unique password and keep it in a password manager or somewhere private. **Don't tell it to me.**
4. Confirm their email if Namecheap asks.

Wait for "done".

## Step 4: Search for and buy the domain (owner does this)

1. Type the top choice into the Namecheap search box and press Search.
2. If it's taken, try the backup.
3. Click **Add to cart**.
4. In the cart, make sure **auto-renew** is on (so the address doesn't expire) and that free domain privacy (WHOIS privacy) is included.
5. Check out themselves. Remind them: I can't see or handle payment details.

Wait for them to say it's purchased.

## Step 5: Build the website

Ask which route they prefer, and recommend the simplest one for their comfort level:

- **Namecheap's own site builder (easiest, connects automatically):** In Namecheap, go to **Domain List**, find the domain, and look for the website builder offer. Follow the prompts.
- **A builder they already know** (Squarespace, Wix, WordPress, Shopify): they can use it and point the domain to it. Ask them to open that builder's help and search "connect a domain from Namecheap"; then coach them through what they see.

Whatever they choose, the site needs these basics. Coach one at a time:

- **Home page:** What the business does, where, and a clear button or phone number.
- **Services or products page.**
- **About page:** a short story and a photo.
- **Contact page:** phone, email, and address (storefront) or service area (travel businesses).
- Use the **exact business name, address (if public), and phone number** that will match their Google Business Profile later. Consistency matters.

## Step 6: Check it works

Ask them to open a new browser tab, type their domain, and tell you what they see.

- If the site loads: great.
- If it says "site can't be reached" or shows a Namecheap parking page: connecting a domain can take from a few minutes up to a few hours. Wait a bit, then retry. If it still fails, ask them what the domain's settings page in the builder says, and troubleshoot with them.

## Done check

Ask: "Your website loads at your own address. Are you done with this step? Say 'done' when ready."

Record the domain name (not any login details) in `my-progress.md`.
