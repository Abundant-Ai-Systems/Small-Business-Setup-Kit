# Guide 4: Social Media Profiles

**Goal:** The business has consistent profiles on the social platforms that matter for them.

**Your role:** Coach only. The owner creates every account themselves. Never ask for passwords, codes, or recovery information.

## Step 1: Choose the platforms

Don't set up everything. Based on the business type from onboarding, recommend two or three:

- **Facebook:** Almost every local business. Good for reviews, hours, and community.
- **Instagram:** Visual businesses (food, beauty, home services with before/after photos, retail, fitness).
- **LinkedIn:** Business-to-business or professional services.
- **YouTube / TikTok:** Only if the owner will actually make video.
- **Nextdoor Business:** Local service businesses in neighborhoods.

Ask which ones they want. Help them settle on a manageable number.

## Step 2: Pick a consistent handle

Aim for the same name everywhere, ideally matching the domain (for example `@bobsplumbing`). Have them check availability on each platform as they sign up. If taken, add a small tweak such as the city (`@bobsplumbingdenver`) and use that same version on all platforms.

## Step 3: Create each profile (owner does this)

Coach one platform at a time and wait for "done" before the next.

**Facebook Page** (not a personal profile):
1. Sign in to their personal Facebook (a Page needs a personal account as its owner), then go to **facebook.com/pages/create**.
2. Enter the business name and category. Add a short description.
3. Add phone, website, hours, and address (or service area).
4. Add a profile picture (logo) and cover photo.

**Instagram:**
1. Create the account in the Instagram app or website using a business email.
2. In **Settings**, switch to a **Professional account** and choose **Business**.
3. Add the website link, phone, and category.

**LinkedIn Company Page:**
1. Sign in to their personal LinkedIn and go to **linkedin.com/company/setup/new**.
2. Fill in name, website, industry, and size.

For other platforms, ask the owner to open the sign-up page and describe what they see, then coach the next click.

## Step 4: Make profiles match

For every profile, check these match the website and Google Business Profile:
- Exact business name
- Phone number
- Website link
- Hours and location or service area
- Same logo and a similar cover image

## Step 5: Link them together

Add the social links to the website (footer or Contact page), and the website link to each profile. Add the social links to the Google Business Profile if the option is available.

## Step 6: First post

Suggest a simple first post: a welcome message, a photo of the owner or team at work, and what the business offers. Keep it friendly and short.

## Security tips (say these out loud)

- Turn on **two-step verification** on every account (they do it in each platform's security settings).
- Use a different strong password on each account, stored in a password manager. Do not share it with me or anyone.

## Done check

Ask: "Are you done with this step? Say 'done' when ready." Record the platforms created (names and public handles only) in `my-progress.md`.
