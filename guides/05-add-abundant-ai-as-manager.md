# Guide 5: Add Abundant AI Systems as a Manager

**Goal:** The owner gives Abundant AI Systems manager-level access to their accounts, so we can help manage them. The owner stays the **primary owner** and can remove our access at any time.

**Your role:** Coach only. The owner sends every invitation from their own accounts. Never ask for passwords. We ask for *invitations*, never logins.

## Our invitation email

**Abundant AI Systems email: `hello@abundantaisystems.com`**

## Step 1: Explain it

Say: "Instead of sharing your password, you can invite us as a manager. You stay the owner. You can remove us any time. Let's do it one account at a time."

Only include the accounts the owner actually has (check `my-progress.md`).

## Step 2: Google Business Profile (owner does this)

1. Go to **business.google.com** and sign in. Open the business profile.
2. Click the three-dot menu or **Settings**, then **People and access** (the wording may be **Business Profile settings** then **Managers**).
3. Click **Add** (the plus or person icon).
4. Enter the Abundant AI Systems email above.
5. Under access, choose **Manager** (not Owner).
6. Click **Invite**.

## Step 3: Search Console (owner does this)

1. Go to **search.google.com/search-console** and pick their website.
2. Click **Settings** (left menu), then **Users and permissions**.
3. Click **Add user**, enter the email, and choose **Full** permission (**Restricted** if they prefer less access).
4. Click **Add**.

## Step 4: Website and domain

- **Namecheap:** Don't share the login. If the owner wants us to help with the domain, suggest they check Namecheap's help page for "Sharing access" and use that. If no sharing option exists, skip this; we can usually work without it.
- **Website builder:** Most builders have **Invite a collaborator** under Settings (look for "Permissions" or "Contributors"). Invite the email with the lowest role that works (Editor or Admin only if needed).

## Step 5: Social media (owner does this)

**Facebook Page:** Go to the Page, then **Settings** > **Page access** (or **Page setup**) > **Add New** > search for the email > choose **Full control** or **Content** access as the owner prefers > confirm.

**Instagram:** Easiest through the Facebook Page connection (Meta Business Suite > Settings > People). Otherwise skip. Don't share the login.

**LinkedIn:** Page admin tools > **Manage admins** > **Add admin** and choose a role.

For anything else, ask the owner to search the platform's help for "add a manager" or "invite a team member".

## Step 6: Confirm

Ask the owner to tell you which invitations they sent (for example, Business Profile, Search Console, Facebook Page). Write that list to `my-progress.md`, with no logins or passwords. Tell the owner:

- Accept the invitation emails if any appear on their side.
- Abundant AI Systems will accept on our end and be in touch.
- They can remove access any time in the same People or Users menus.

## Done check

Ask: "Are you done with this step? Say 'done' when ready." Then move to Part 5 of `ONBOARDING.md`.
