# Foundation for Google and AI Search

**A free guided setup for your website, Google Business Profile, Search Console, and social profiles.**

Made by [Abundant AI Systems](mailto:hello@abundantaisystems.com) for small business owners. No tech skills needed. An AI assistant walks you through each step in plain English.

## What you need

- A computer (Mac or Windows)
- The Claude Code app, which needs a paid Claude plan
- About 30 to 60 minutes

**Prefer a printable version?** [Download the Setup Guide (PDF)](Setup-Guide.pdf), a 6-page walkthrough of everything below.

## How it works: three steps

### 1. Install Claude Code

Go to [claude.com/download](https://claude.com/download), download the Claude app, and sign in with your paid Claude plan. Open the **Code** tab.

### 2. Paste the setup box

Copy everything in the box below, paste it into Claude Code, and press Enter.

```
Please download the Foundation for Google and AI Search from
https://github.com/Abundant-Ai-Systems/Small-Business-Setup-Kit
into my Documents folder, then open it and follow the instructions in
ONBOARDING.md. Ask me one question at a time, explain every step in plain
English, and never ask me for any passwords.
```

### 3. Answer the questions

Claude will ask about your business, find out what you already have, and make a short checklist of what's missing. Then it guides you through each setup, one step at a time. It only moves on when you say "done". You can stop any time and pick up later.

## What we'll set up

1. Your website and web address (domain), through Namecheap
2. Google Search Console, which shows how people find your website
3. Your Google Business Profile, so you show up on Google Search and Maps
4. Your social media profiles
5. Adding Abundant AI Systems as a manager (the last step, and your choice)

## You stay in control

- **You create and verify every account yourself.** Google requires it, and it keeps your accounts yours. Claude only coaches: it tells you what to click and what you should see.
- **Claude never asks for your passwords.** Keep them private. If you ever feel tempted to share one in the chat, don't.
- **Adding us as manager is the very last step.** You invite Abundant AI Systems as a manager, never as the owner, and you can remove us at any time.
